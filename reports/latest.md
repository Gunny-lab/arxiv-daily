**Abstract**

We investigate non-invertible completions of anomalous flavor symmetries and their application to Dirac neutrino masses. We formulate symmetry defects dressed by topological quantum field theories for flavor symmetries including finite non-Abelian flavor transformations with rational anomaly characters and modular flavor symmetries with the rational part of modular anomaly cocycles, for gauge sectors with the required exact higher-form symmetry. As a concrete example, we construct a Froggatt-Nielsen (FN) model in which an order-four subgroup $\mathbb Z_4^{\rm FN}\subset U(1)_{\rm FN}$ is completed into a non-invertible defect through its mixed anomaly with an intermediate hidden $U(1)_H$ gauge sector in the infrared monopole-decoupling limit. The non-invertible FN symmetry can be explicitly broken at the energy scale where monopoles become dynamical. This breaking from the infrared viewpoint can be realized in an ultraviolet hidden $SU(2)_H$ theory as instanton effects, where a constrained instanton with topological charge one generates a fermion mixing term that violates FN charge by one unit. Its coefficient contains the nonperturbative factor $ζ_H\sim\exp[-8π^2/g_H^2(v_H)]$. When independent FN-breaking terms are subleading, integrating out the heavy fermions gives $(Y_ν)_{ij}\proptoζ_Hε^{\ell_i+n_j}$ in a rotated basis for the right-handed neutrinos, to leading order in the instanton-induced fermion mixing. The nonperturbative factor provides the exponential suppression of the neutrino Yukawa couplings, while the FN charges determine the powers of $ε$. For the chosen FN charges and generic order-one coefficients, $m_2/m_3\simε$, favoring normal ordering. An independent $\mathbb Z_8^D$ gauge symmetry forbids Majorana masses. The same FN charge assignment reproduces the leading quark and charged-lepton Yukawa hierarchies.

**Abstract**

We study the connection between the $SU(N)_{K}$ Wess-Zumino-Witten (WZW) model and $SU(N)_{K'}$ WZW models, enforcing the unbroken $\mathbb{Z}_{N}$ symmetries. The fundamental underlying structures are the (half-)integer spin simple currents in the topological quantum field theories and anomaly matching conditions in conformal field theories. **Abstract**

Under the channel positivity conditions, we obtain an infrared-finite positivity bound on local four-scalar Wilson coefficients that includes a finite gravitational correction of order $G_N$ and is derived from the full UV absorptive spectrum without specifying a UV theory, imposing a spectral ansatz, requiring weak coupling or expanding the spectrum perturbatively. The framework opens a route to infrared-finite constraints on effective interactions across particle physics and gravity.

**Abstract**

This suggests that the smooth vacuum polarization of the genuine four-dimensional DSS Choptuik spacetime may similarly be governed by its exact echoing symmetry.

We select external deformations that preserve the independent quantum residues and lift them by varying the internal moduli so that the independent perturbative quantum periods remain fixed. The Riemann bilinear identity then determines the induced evolution of the non-perturbative periods through local residue pairings and the inverse period Jacobian. At genus one, this reduces to a relation between the flow of the non-perturbative quantum period and the energy derivative with respect to the perturbative action, providing a constructive connection between the Rayleigh-Schrödinger expansion and fluctuations around non-perturbative saddles. We recover known P/NP relations and derive extensions for explicitly coupling-dependent potentials, which we verify at the computed orders through independent perturbative and non-perturbative period calculations for quantum-corrected quartic and Jacobi elliptic examples.
