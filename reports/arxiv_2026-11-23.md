### Two Faces of Spacetime Density Matrices: Entanglement and Complexity

- Relevance score: 34 (high)
- Scoop-risk score: 20/100 (normal)
- Scoop-risk reasons: same_question: entanglement
- Matched keywords: title: entanglement; abstract: amplitude, entanglement

**Abstract**

We establish a direct connection between entanglement and complexity through spacetime density matrices. For stationary pure states and complementary regions at different times, spacetime replica moments encode the return amplitude of a replica-defect quench. This representation defines an associated state spread complexity, which we call Krylov-Rényi complexity. It can equivalently be obtained by using a replica-defect excitation of the replicated background state as the seed and evolving it under the original replicated Hamiltonian. We further construct Krylov-von Neumann complexity as a probe limit from first-order replica data. Timelike entanglement and complexity provide complementary information at a fixed time, while the full time dependence of the timelike entropy determines the Krylov dynamics and hence the complexity. The replica construction fixes the seed and its evolution through the background state, subsystem geometry, and regulator, without an independently chosen probe operator. Like entanglement entropy, the resulting complexity is defined nonperturbatively and probes universal properties and dynamical information about the underlying theory.

**Abstract**

We investigate the black hole phase diagram of five-dimensional minimal supergravity using the blackfold approach and the framework of generalized symmetries. Starting from supergravity coupled to probe sources, we derive the 2-group (non-)conservation laws, construct the equilibrium action for a two-dimensional 2-group fluid, and compute the associated global conserved charges. We then match the thermodynamics of the general non-extremal black string in the ungauged theory to that of this fluid, organizing the blackfold effective theory and its solutions in terms of symmetry invariants. Finally, we apply the same framework to construct extremal black rings in AdS$_5$, identifying three regular branches that are promising candidates for an exact completion.

**Abstract**

Tensor network renormalization at finite bond dimension $χ$ introduces truncation errors that are amplified by the renormalization-group (RG) flow. For loop tensor network renormalization (LoopTNR) on the $c=1$ critical line of the six-vertex model, with Luttinger parameter $K$, we identify these errors operator by operator from symmetry-resolved transfer-matrix spectra and conformal perturbation theory. A larger $χ$ lowers the initial amplitude of the error for $0.75\le K\le 1.5$ but changes neither the operator nor its growth rate.

**Abstract**

We propose the three-dimensional Skyrme model as a topological benchmark for neural variational methods and construct fully three-dimensional Skyrmions with baryon number $B=1,\ldots,4$ without imposing a symmetry ansatz, with high precision. For $B=2,3,4$, the relaxation of these Skyrmions starts from $B$ unit-charge lumps with random positions and orientations, which carry no symmetry information. The known toroidal, tetrahedral, and cubic Skyrmions nevertheless emerge spontaneously. The resulting variational energies for $B=1,\ldots,4$ lie slightly below the published reference values. The neural method highlights the high accuracy for retaining the topological charge, differing from $B$ by at most $10^{-6}$ in the highest-precision solutions. Our numerical method goes through physical constraint tests such as the Derrick virial relation and the Faddeev--Bogomolny bound, together with evaluation / verification through independent quadratures and resolution sweeps. We then compare Kolmogorov--Arnold networks (KANs) with multilayer perceptrons (MLPs) in more than a thousand training runs, both with and without a rational-map ansatz. With default settings, KANs appear markedly superior, but most of the gap disappears once the MLP activation function and learning rate are tuned. KANs remain more parameter-efficient and more robust at fixed epoch budget, while MLPs with the tuned activation function perform at least as well at fixed wall-clock time. We also argue that continuous gradient descent cannot smoothly change the topological sector of our neural field, clarifying the origin of topological freezing observed in our neural variational solvers. Our results on the neural methods applied to Skyrmions pave a research ground for AI-agentic benchmarks for classical topological solitons, toward AI-assisted soliton physics.

**Abstract**

We study absolute upper bounds on the mass ratio $R_{11}=m_{r_1}/m_{h_1}$ between the radion ($r_1$) and graviton ($h_1$) lightest modes in five dimensional warped models. We consider warped models with an admissible singular behavior in the infrared: i.e.~models with power-like ($\sim \barφ^{2n}$) and with exponential ($\sim e^{2ν\barφ}$) bulk potentials, where $\barφ$ is the stabilizing bulk field. We find a correlation between $R_{11}$ and the singular behavior of the Ricci scalar, which allows to establish an absolute upper bound on the former. For the power-like models, the maximum value of $R_{11}$ increases with $n$. For exponential models we also obtain the absolute upper bound on the second radion mode ratio $R_{21}=m_{r_2}/m_{h_1}\lesssim 1.374$. This value is reached for $ν=2$ when the IR brane approaches the singularity. For this configuration, we verify that the scalar contribution satisfies the unitarity sum rule when the complete radion Kaluza-Klein tower is included.

**Abstract**

We develop a microscopic and thermodynamically consistent framework for relativistic equilibrium spin magnetohydrodynamics of massless chiral fermions, directly from first-order chiral kinetic theory. Starting from the covariant Wigner function, we recast the chiral kinetic equation as a pure streaming equation on a magnetically shifted mass shell, incorporating both the magnetic-moment energy and the Berry-curvature phase-space measure. This formulation yields a kinetic $H$-theorem and an entropy current with vanishing divergence in global equilibrium. We construct the equilibrium distribution for an arbitrary spin frame and derive the associated currents, energy--momentum tensor, spin tensor, entropy current, and frame-independent spin polarization, including the magnetic splitting between particles and antiparticles. Thermodynamic consistency further determines the second-order magnetovortical responses, revealing a relativistic Barnett magnetization, a temperature- and chemical-potential-independent anomalous charge density, and rotation-induced density corrections that reproduce exact field-theory results. Our framework provides a unified microscopic foundation for relativistic equilibrium spin magnetohydrodynamics and establishes a direct link between quantum kinetic theory, spin polarization, magnetization, and anomalous transport.

**Abstract**

A question of fundamental interest is whether parity is violated on large scales in the Universe. This possibility has been motivated by recent analyses of the four-point matter correlation function. It has also been suggested that detectable parity violation could be generated during inflation by the dynamical Chern-Simons operator, a leading effective field theory correction to general relativity. In this work, we examine this possibility in realistic UV completions, including fermionic and string-theoretic examples. Thus, while a future search for large scale parity violation is worth pursuing, generating a detectable signal within a consistent effective theory is highly nontrivial.

**Abstract**

We exhibit random matrix statistics in the scaling dimensions of non-BPS primary operators in a family of two-dimensional conformal field theories.
